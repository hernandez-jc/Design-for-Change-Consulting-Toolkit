## Why this review matters

The Moodle learning ecosystem supports learner capability, operational readiness, and organizational change. When learners cannot easily find, access, understand, or apply learning, the organization may experience lower adoption, repeated support requests, unclear compliance visibility, or reduced confidence in the learning environment.

This review focused on a practical first cycle: understanding the current state, identifying the most important barriers, and selecting improvements that can be delivered or meaningfully advanced within 10 working days.

---

## What we assessed

The review covered eight areas:

1. Strategy and business alignment  
2. Learners and audiences  
3. Content and learning design  
4. Administration and usability  
5. Data and reporting  
6. Accessibility and inclusion  
7. Integrations and interoperability  
8. Governance and change readiness  

The assessment used stakeholder discovery, available Moodle reports, content examples, and the Learning Checkup Scorecard.

## Expected outcome

At the end of the 10-day cycle, the organization should have:

- A clearer understanding of its current Moodle learning ecosystem.
- A small number of practical improvements completed or underway.
- Clear ownership for the selected actions.
- Simple evidence of whether the improvements are working.
- A documented list of deferred items for the next cycle.
- A foundation for ongoing, evidence-informed improvement.

## Recommendation

Proceed with the selected 10-day roadmap.

The selected actions focus on practical improvements that support learner adoption, administrator usability, content quality, accessibility, and change readiness. They are scoped to respect current constraints while creating visible value and a basis for future improvement.
