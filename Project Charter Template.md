## 7. Scope

### In scope

- [ ] Discovery with key stakeholders
- [ ] Review of the current Moodle learning ecosystem
- [ ] Assessment of learner adoption and administrator usability
- [ ] Review of priority content quality and accessibility
- [ ] Identification of data, reporting, integration, and governance needs
- [ ] Prioritization of improvements
- [ ] Creation of a 10-day roadmap
- [ ] Preparation of an executive brief

### Out of scope

- [ ] Full organizational change-management program
- [ ] Complete redesign of all courses or content
- [ ] Custom plugin development
- [ ] LMS migration
- [ ] Detailed technical architecture design
- [ ] Legal review of privacy or regulatory obligations
- [ ] Moodle Workplace implementation
- [ ] Procurement of new platforms or major systems
